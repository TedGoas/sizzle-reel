# Ted voice (prototype microcopy)

This is the short version for product UI in sizzle-reel. It is not the long-form article skill from the Dante repo.

## Rules

- Plain, specific words. Say what the screen is doing.
- Short sentences. One idea each.
- Sentence case for labels and headings.
- Oxford comma.
- No em dashes or en dashes. Use commas, periods, parentheses, or colons. Ranges use “to” (“5 to 10 minutes”).
- Match or shorten the length Ted asked for. Do not add a paragraph of explanation under a button.
- Skip corporate polish: “delve,” “unlock,” “in today’s world,” “cutting-edge,” “seamless,” “elevate,” “leverage.”
- First person only when the product is speaking as itself (“We’ll notify you when it’s done”). Do not narrate the design.

## How to hand it back

Give the string that goes in the component. If a few wordings are plausible, give two or three labeled options and stop. Do not explain the voice unless asked.

## Example

Working toast, while a test run is in progress:

> This should take 5 to 10 minutes. It’s ok to leave this page. We’ll notify you when it’s done.
